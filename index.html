Privacy Policy

TILTED — neon pinball for Android (package com.tilted)
Effective date: November 21, 2026

The short version: TILTED does not require an account and does not collect, sell, or share your personal information with the developer. Your game progress stays on your device. The app shows optional rewarded video ads served by Google AdMob, and Google may process device data to deliver those ads as described below.

1. Who we are

TILTED is developed and published by Mcm190 ("we", "us"). For any privacy question, contact us at 180624855+Mcm190@users.noreply.github.com.

2. Data stored by the game

The game saves your scores, settings, and progress locally on your device only. This data is never uploaded to us or to any server we operate. We do not run analytics, crash reporting, or tracking SDKs of our own, and the game has no sign-in, chat, or user-generated content features. Uninstalling the app removes all locally stored game data.

3. Advertising (Google AdMob)

TILTED offers optional rewarded video ads: you choose to watch an ad to earn extra balls. Ads are provided by Google AdMob (Google LLC). When the ads SDK is active, Google may automatically collect and process information such as:

  • Advertising ID and other device identifiers
  • IP address (used to estimate coarse location)
  • Device and app information (model, OS version, app version, language)
  • Ad interaction data (impressions, clicks, reward completion) and diagnostics
>This data is processed by Google, not by us, and is used to serve, measure, and (where you consent) personalize ads, and to prevent fraud. Google's handling of this data is governed by:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses data in AdMob apps

4. Your consent and choices

Where required by law (for example in the European Economic Area and the UK), the app shows Google's consent form before any ads are loaded, and ads only run after consent is obtained or where consent is not required. You can also:

  • Manage ad personalization at Google Ad Settings
  • Reset or delete your advertising ID in Android settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or equivalent)
  • Simply decline to watch rewarded ads — the game is fully playable without them

5. Children's privacy

TILTED is a general-audience game and is not directed at children under 13 (or the equivalent minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you are a parent or guardian and believe your child has provided personal information through the app's advertising, please contact us and we will help you request its deletion from the relevant provider.

6. Data retention and deletion

We retain no personal data on our own servers — there is nothing for us to delete. Local game data is removed when you uninstall the app. For data processed by Google for advertising, see the Google policies linked above and your device's ad settings.

7. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version is always published at this page, with the effective date shown at the top. Material changes will be reflected in an updated effective date.
